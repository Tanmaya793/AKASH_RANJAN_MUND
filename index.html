The Athlete Journey

Kalahandi to the World Stage

Hailing from the district of Bhawanipatna, Kalahandi, Odisha, Akash Ranjan Mund represents the pinnacle of perseverance in Indian para-sports. Known as the "Golden Boy" of Indian Para Badminton, he has overcome structural and physical barriers to represent India globally in the SL3 classification.

Currently pursuing his BA in English at the School of Liberal Studies, KIIT Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, Akash balances academic pursuits with a grueling training regime under sports legends to keep the tricolor flying high.

BWF & World Abilitysport Recognition

Para-badminton is fully recognized by the Badminton World Federation (BWF). The World Abilitysport Youth Games serve as the peak international arena for discovering and testing elite youth para-athletes worldwide.
