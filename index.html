The Journey

Kalahandi to the World Stage

Hailing from Bhawanipatna, Kalahandi, Odisha, Akash Ranjan Mund represents the pinnacle of perseverance in Indian para-sports. Known as the "Golden Boy" of Indian Para Badminton, he has overcome structural barriers to represent India in BWF international SL3 competitions, training under Dronacharya Awardee Gaurav Khanna. He balances his rigorous training with his studies at KIIT Deemed to be University.
